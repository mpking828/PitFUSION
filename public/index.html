Help

Data Integrity

PitFUSION fuses data from two independent sources — The Blue Alliance and Nexus FRC — each with its own update schedule, data model, and definition of "current." We reconcile these as best we can, but they don't always agree.

Queuing status, bumper colors, and next-match predictions are most likely to drift during Playoffs and Finals, where bracket assignments change in real time and Nexus may briefly stop reporting while a match is in progress.

PitFUSION is a situational awareness tool. Always confirm match assignments and bumper colors with your drive coach or the queuing staff at the field.

Resizing panels

Drag the divider bars between panels to resize them on the fly — the sidebar, the stream, and the match list all adjust. Double-click any divider to snap the layout back to its default.

Themes

Open ⚙ Settings → Appearance to switch between Dark, Light, TJ², and Custom. Your choice is remembered on this display.

Pick Custom, then Edit Custom theme… to set your own colours, a background image (a URL or an uploaded file), and frosted-panel styling. Changes preview live; press Save to keep them on this browser. Copy CSS exports the block for baking into a self-hosted index.html.
